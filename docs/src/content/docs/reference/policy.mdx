Hostwright evaluates policy before runtime effects. Decisions identify a
category, stable reason code, severity, operator explanation, and remediation.
The current policy surface covers Manifest v3 validation, resource admission,
provider capability, ownership, image and secret rules, exposure, cleanup, and
recovery.

## Core rules

- Unsupported manifest fields and provider behaviors fail before effects.
- Capacity requests and limits are validated and reserved through local
  scheduler authority.
- Broad host exposure and unsafe mount or secret handling are blocked.
- Cleanup requires a current plan and exact proof of Hostwright ownership.
- Team profiles can add stricter requirements. They cannot bypass built-in
  safety gates or grant broader mutation authority.

Policy decisions explain and constrain behavior; they do not themselves run a
runtime command, write state, or contact a registry. Commands enforce their
own authenticated effect and recovery boundaries.

For exact rules and reason codes, see the core
[policy reference](https://github.com/hostwright/hostwright/blob/main/docs/reference/policy.md)
and [Limitations](/reference/limitations/).
